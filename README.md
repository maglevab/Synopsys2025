**Human Vs. Artificial: Precision & Recall in AI-Generated Text Detection with Expert LLMS.**

**Awarded : 2nd place Category Award for Software Engineering (from 100’s of entries)**

**Context:**
Witnessing classmates use AI to complete assignments undetected while teachers struggled with unreliable detection tools no better than a coin toss, I developed AuthenText, a specialized detection system that achieves over 93% accuracy across academic, news, and literary text to restore academic integrity. Over the course of 6 months, I independently designed and developed a robust solution to address this problem.. **Abstract:**

The release of Generative-AI has unleashed an unprecedented wave of AI-generated content, permeating academic and scientific writing, literature, news, and social media. With this growth, the challenge of detecting a piece of content as human or AI-generated has increased. Current solutions are at best a “coin toss” and often work only for one type of text, e.g., academic essays. My solution, AuthenText, is a modular, universal, AI-generated text detection software that consists of three components: a text classifier, an expert model detector and multiple synthesizers. AuthenText focuses on three critical categories of text: Academic/Scientific, News/Social Media, and Literature. The text classifier, a Generative LLM programmed with a prompt, classifies the text into one of the three categories. Each expert model is a fine-tuned LLM, trained in one of three specific categories to detect AI-Generated text. The synthesizers consist of three Logistic Regression models which learn to intelligently weigh expert model responses differently depending on the text category, accurately condensing the responses into a final response. To analyze the AuthenText’s results, I used standard statistics metrics including precision, recall, and accuracy. AuthenText had a 93.6% precision, 92% recall, and a 93.4% accuracy. In addition to this, there was only a 4.6% difference in precision and 3% difference in recall across the three categories. By reliably detecting AI-generated text across many categories, AuthenText represents a massive step forward in creating a universal detector that transforms the way individuals and organizations discern AI-generated text of any type.
